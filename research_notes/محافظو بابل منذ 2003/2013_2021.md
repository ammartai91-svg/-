## Sadiq Madloul (صادق مدلول السلطاني): start and end dates

### Takeaway
Only a Wikipedia-derived mirror says he was elected governor on 20 April 2013 (that is the election date, not necessarily the vote for governor); no end date found. SINGLE-SOURCE / UNCONFIRMED.

### Cited Findings
- Babil governorate election held 20 April 2013; State of Law 8 seats, Citizen Alliance 7, Sadrist bloc 4 (summary disagrees on council total, 24 vs 31). Previous governor before the election was Salman Hassan al-Zarkani (Sadrist). — [Wikipedia mirror via search](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_Babil_governorate_election) (SINGLE-SOURCE; page not opened)
- A Wikipedia-derived page lists "Sadiq Madlool Hamad Jasim (Elected April 20, 2013; 6th Governor of Babylon)", with Wisam Asslan as deputy governor. — [Babil Governorate mirror](https://ipfs.ssi.eecc.de/ipns/en.wikipedia-on-ipfs.org/wiki/Babil_Governorate) (SINGLE-SOURCE; mirror may be outdated)
- Later (not 2013): a Shafaq item says Babel council elected "Sadeq al-Madlol" from State of Law as governor; year not shown (possibly confusion with a different period). — [Shafaq EN via search](https://shafaq.com/en/Iraq/Political-Blocs-in-Babel-Provincial-Council-reject-appointment-of-Adnan-Al-Faihan-as-governor) (UNCONFIRMED, undated)

## Between Madloul and Karrar al-Abadi; al-Abadi's term and dismissal

### Takeaway
Karrar al-Abadi was dismissed "in October" (year not stated in snippet; the user's hypothesis is Sept/Oct 2019). A Babil council member lists successive governors as al-Abadi, Mandil, Wisam Aslan, but that is one member's list, not an official record.

### Cited Findings
- Rudaw: arrest warrant for "former Babil governor" Karrar al-Abadi on corruption (Hilla Investigation Court, forged land grants to martyrs' families/political prisoners), article 240320215 (appears to be 24 March 2021 or 2024 by URL pattern; date UNCONFIRMED); says he was removed in October (year not given); in March 2020 Babel Criminal Court sentenced him to 7 years for forging identity papers used to become governor, never jailed. — [Rudaw](https://rudaw.net/english/middleeast/iraq/240320215) (SINGLE-SOURCE, via search summary)
- Shafaq "خراب ومحنة": council member Mohammed al-Mansouri says Babil was afflicted by four governors in the past decade, several with corruption/forgery cases; the summary lists al-Abadi, then Hassan Mandil, then Wisam Aslan. Report dated ~Aug 2024 per summary. — [Shafaq](https://shafaq.com/ar/تقارير-وتحليلات/خراب-ومحنة-محافظة-بابل-عقد-من-ال-همال-دون-محاسبة-ومحافظون-سابقون-متورطون-بالفساد) (page not opened)
- Wisam Aslan (وسام أصلان): described in 2021 Shafaq reporting as FIRST DEPUTY governor, not governor. Also faced forgery/corruption allegations. — [Shafaq](https://shafaq.com/ar/أمـن/مس-ول-محلي-يرهن-عودة-سكان-جرف-الصخر-بتحقيق-شرط) (SINGLE-SOURCE)
- I found NO source giving al-Abadi's start date, the exact dismissal date, or who held the post immediately before him (Madloul or another).

### Inferences
- Whether Wisam Aslan was ever a (acting) governor is UNCONFIRMED; the council member's list suggests he may have acted at some point (possibly after Mandil's May 2022 dismissal, which is outside scope), not between Madloul and al-Abadi.

## Hassan Mandil (حسن منديل)

### Takeaway
Mandil was governor in an acting (assigned) capacity: Shafaq (May 2022) calls him "acting governor" whom al-Kadhimi relieved while keeping him as deputy governor. His start date was not found. By April 2021 he was governor and faced an arrest warrant.

### Cited Findings
- May 2022: Kadhimi relieved Mandil as governor and kept him as deputy governor; Coordination Framework, Sadrists, Imtidad agreed on tax director Ali Waad Allawi as successor; protests against Mandil over alleged corruption/mismanagement. — [Shafaq AR](https://shafaq.com/ar/سیاسة/الكاظمي-يعفي-منديل-من-منصبه-ويبقيه-بمنصب-نا-ب-محافظ-بابل) and [Shafaq EN "acting governor"](https://api.shafaq.com/en/Iraq/Al-Kadhimi-dismisses-Babel-s-governor-heralding-a-new-power-sharing-formula-source) (same agency; SINGLE-SOURCE)
- April 2021: Shafaq reported a security force raided governor Hassan Mandil's house after an arrest warrant for him, his son and son-in-law; a later report said a special force arrested "Hassan Mandil Al-Sariawi". — [Shafaq](https://shafaq.com/en/Iraq/Babel-s-governor-disappears-after-an-arrest-warrant-issued-against-him) and [arrest report](https://shafaq.com/amp/en/Iraq/A-special-force-from-Baghdad-arrests-Babel-s-Governor) (dates per search summary)
- Later in 2022 Mandil returned to duties; Babil MPs objected for lack of a Cabinet letter; he said he did not appoint himself and awaited PM al-Sudani's decision. — [Shafaq](https://shafaq.com/ar/سیاسة/منديل-يوضح-لنواب-بابل-لم-اكلف-نفسي-وانتظر-قرار-السوداني)

### Gaps
