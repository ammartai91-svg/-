## Q1: Hassan Mandil / Ali Waad Allawi in 2022; who governed mid-2022 to the Dec 2023 elections

### Takeaway
Kadhimi relieved Mandil in late May 2022 (he stayed deputy governor) and tasked Ali Waad Allawi; later, after Sudani cancelled the outgoing government's orders, Mandil says he resumed work, but I found no resolution and nothing at all covering 2023 up to Feb 2024.

### Cited Findings
- Kadhimi relieved Mandil of governor duties, keeping him as deputy governor, and tasked Ali Waad Allawi with running the province; Arabic Shafaq report dated 2022-05-28 per search summary — [Shafaq AR](https://shafaq.com/ar/سیاسة/الكاظمي-يعفي-منديل-من-منصبه-ويبقيه-بمنصب-نا-ب-محافظ-بابل); related [Shafaq EN](https://www.shafaq.com/en/Iraq/Al-Kadhimi-dismisses-Babel-s-governor-heralding-a-new-power-sharing-formula-source)
- Allawi (Babil tax director) was agreed by the Fatah (Amiri) and Sadrist alliances; he had 7 of 17 Babil MPs' signatures — [Shafaq](https://shafaq.com/ar/سیاسة/تحالفا-العامري-والصدر-يتفقان-على-مرشح-لمنصب-ر-يس-الحكومة-المحلية-في-بابل)
- Mandil, per Shafaq, said he handed the governorate to Allawi on 29/5/2022 (completed in half an hour); later said Cabinet decision 280 of 2022 cancelled the diwani orders issued since 8 Oct 2021, including Allawi's, so his own dismissal was void; he awaited PM Sudani's decision; Babil MPs objected to him resuming without an official Cabinet letter — [Shafaq](https://shafaq.com/ar/سیاسة/منديل-يوضح-لنواب-بابل-لم-اكلف-نفسي-وانتظر-قرار-السوداني). Page date not shown; Sudani reference implies after late Oct 2022 (search summary said Nov 2022, unverified).
- Sudani cancelled many appointments of the previous government — [Al-Monitor](https://www.al-monitor.com/originals/2022/11/sudani-removes-head-iraqi-intelligence-other-officials) (general context, not Babil-specific)

### Inferences
- Allawi's tenure likely ran ~29 May to late 2022 and ended via cancellation of Kadhimi-era orders; Mandil probably resumed as governor but this is only his own account.

### Gaps
- Who was governor from late 2022 through the Dec 2023 elections and until Fayhan's Feb election: not found. Mandil's party and final departure date not found. Whether Allawi or Mandil was formally confirmed by Sudani: not found. Need to check Iraqi Cabinet decision 280/2022 and Babil press for 2023.

## Q2: Adnan Fayhan al-Dulaimi

### Takeaway
Council selected Fayhan (head of Sadiqoun bloc) on 6 Feb (WINEP; year not in snippet, but he was governor by May 2024); he left on being elected First Deputy Speaker on 29 Dec 2025; first deputy governor Alaa Al-Araji acted.

### Cited Findings
- On 6 Feb the Babil council chose Adnan Fayhan al-Dulaymi, head of the Sadiqoun parliamentary bloc, as governor — [Washington Institute](https://www.washingtoninstitute.org/policy-analysis/aah-terrorist-planner-adnan-fayhan-takes-over-babil-governor); several council blocs opposed the appointment — [Shafaq EN](https://shafaq.com/en/Iraq/Political-Blocs-in-Babel-Provincial-Council-reject-appointment-of-Adnan-Al-Faihan-as-governor)
- Fayhan was governor on 2 May 2024 (met Ammar al-Hakim) — [ammaralhakeem.com](https://ammaralhakeem.com/ar/news/pdf/18299)
- Fayhan elected First Deputy Speaker 29 Dec 2025, 177 votes vs 107 for Mohsen al-Mandalawi — [Shafaq AR](https://shafaq.com/ar/سیاسة/ال-طار-التنسيقي-يتفق-على-ترشيح-عدنان-فيحان-نا-با-ولا-لر-يس-البرلمان); [Shafaq EN](https://shafaq.com/en/Iraq/Coordination-Framework-settles-on-Iraq-s-first-deputy-speaker)
