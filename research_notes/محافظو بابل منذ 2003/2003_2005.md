## Who was the first governor after April 2003, and when exactly?

### Takeaway
Iskandar Witwit (Arabic: اسكندر وتوت; Elaph gives the longer form "أسكنر جواد وتوت", a retired army major-general) was the first post-invasion governor, installed around early April 2003. Exact start date not established.

### Cited Findings
- Witwit was installed as governor in early April 2003, as US forces captured Hillah (search summary of Washington Times, 10 Dec 2003; page not opened) — [Washington Times](https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2003/dec/10/20031210-123023-6201r/). SINGLE-SOURCE for the date; appointment by US forces/CPA is implied, not stated.
- Alsumaria-derived summary: Witwit held the governor post after the April 2003 occupation, under US/CPA tutelage, and later became "first deputy governor" — [Alsumaria](https://www.alsumaria.tv/news/security/22799/%D9%87%D9%8A%D8%A6%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B3%D8%A7%D8%A1%D9%84%D8%A9-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D8%B5%D8%A7%D8%AF%D9%82-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B7%D8%B9%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B0%D9%8A-%D8%AA%D9%82%D8%AF%D9%85-%D8%A8%D9%87-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%A6%D8%A8-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%88%D9%84-%D9%84%D9%85%D8%AD%D8%A7%D9%81) (Arabic spelling اسكندر وتوت confirmed in this and several other Alsumaria headlines). Spelling is CONFIRMED across Alsumaria/Elaph/Al-Watan Voice items.
- Another report (Al-Sabah, via search snippet) says Witwit's first governorship was in 2003 under occupation and lasted until 2005 — [Al-Sabah](https://alsabaah.iq/60137-.html); CONFLICTS with the December 2003 dismissal below.
- Al-Watan Voice piece by Col. Hamid al-Wasiti calls him "اللواء الركن المُجتث اسكندر جواد وتوت" (maj.-gen., de-Baathified) — [Al-Watan Voice](https://pulpit.alwatanvoice.com/articles/2010/02/14/189251.html).

## Dismissal / succession, and any other governors up to end 2005

### Takeaway
Sources conflict. One line: Witwit removed Dec 2003 by the Governing Council (de-Baathification) -> unnamed successor(s) -> possible 2005 changes. Names floated in search summaries (Imad Lefta Merdan, Abdulrahman Mustafa Fatah, Salim/Salam al-Saleh/Salih al-Mesalmawi) are all UNCONFIRMED for specific dates.

### Cited Findings
- Dec 2003: the pro-US Babil governor was fired on orders of the Iraqi Governing Council over suspected Baath ties (name not in snippet, but context = Witwit) — [Washington Times](https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2003/dec/10/20031210-123023-6201r/). SINGLE-SOURCE.
- Elaph archive: the de-Baathification commission (headed by Ahmad Chalabi) decided to remove the Babil governor "أسكنر جواد وتوت"; residents had demonstrated demanding his removal — [Elaph](https://elaph.com/Web/Archive/1070986949560433400.htm). Archive ID suggests early Dec 2003 (inference). Second source on the Dec 2003 removal -> removal itself is reasonably CONFIRMED (2 sources, snippets only), exact date UNCONFIRMED.
- IWPR (snippet): Governor Witwit resigned three days into protests (Feb 2005) and the CPA replaced him with ex-air-force officer Imad Lefta Merdan — [IWPR](https://iwpr.net/5vg9flbq). SINGLE-SOURCE, internally odd (CPA dissolved June 2004), and contradicts the Dec 2003 firing; do not use without opening the page. Arabic spelling of Merdan (عماد لفتة مردان?) is a guess; Arabic search found nothing.
- English Wikipedia-derived pages (via snippet): council re-elected "Abdulrahman Mustapha Fatah" as governor, serving since 2003 invasion; infobox of 2005 Babil council election lists Salim al-Mesalmaoui (ISCI) as subsequent governor — [Wikipedia 2005 Babil election](https://en.wikipedia.com/wiki/2005_Babil_governorate_council_election). Appear inconsistent; UNCONFIRMED. Arabic forms unverified (سالم/سليم المسلماوي?).
- A 2008 US cable names Governor Salam Salah Mahdi al-Muslimawi (later period, outside scope) — [Scoop/Wikileaks](https://www.scoop.co.nz/stories/WL0804/S00657.htm); shows the Muslimawi governor is post-2005 elections (likely from 2005/06), not an occupation-period appointee.
- A 2005 candidate list reportedly names "Salim Salih Mahdi" as Babil governor (unspecified source in search summary) — UNCONFIRMED.
- Context: Fadil Mahmud Gharib (Baath-era governor) was arrested 15 May 2003 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fadil_Mahmud_Gharib); pre-occupation, not in scope.

### Inferences
- Likely chain (unverified): Witwit (Apr-Dec 2003) -> a Governing Council/CPA-era replacement (name unknown; Abdulrahman Mustafa Fatah is a candidate) -> Muslimawi after Jan 2005 council election (took office roughly spring 2005, date unverified). Merdan's role is doubtful.

### Gaps
- Name, date, and appointment mechanism of the governor from Dec 2003 to the Jan 2005 election; whether any acting governor existed; exact start of the first elected-council governor.
